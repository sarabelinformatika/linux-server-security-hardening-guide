# Limitations

- This guide is distribution-aware but not a substitute for the exact vendor documentation for a supported release.
- Package names, defaults, crypto policy, kernel settings, service units, and lifecycle dates differ by release and hosting model.
- Containers, Kubernetes nodes, appliances, real-time systems, GPUs, high-performance computing, and immutable hosts require additional profiles.
- The read-only scripts provide point-in-time evidence and can miss namespaces, containers, transient units, cloud controls, or restricted state.
- A benchmark score does not prove resistance to attack, regulatory compliance, or business suitability.
- FIPS mode, Secure Boot, TPM policy, full-disk encryption, and MAC enforcement require platform-specific engineering and recovery tests.
- Cloud firewalls, IAM, metadata services, images, disks, snapshots, and provider logs remain in scope even when host controls are strong.
- Never apply examples without testing, rollback, console access, and workload-owner approval.
